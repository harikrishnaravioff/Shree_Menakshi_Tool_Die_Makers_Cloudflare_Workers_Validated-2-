01 / Rubber

Rubber Moulds & Tools

Compression, transfer and injection moulding solutions for industrial rubber components, with tooling designed around repeatability, flash control and dimensional requirements.

  • Compression moulds
  • Transfer moulds
  • Injection moulds
  • O-ring moulds
  • Rubber seal moulds
  • Gasket moulds
  • Bush moulds
  • Diaphragm moulds
  • Valve-seat moulds
  • Custom rubber component moulds

Materials: EPDM, NBR, HNBR, FKM/Viton, Silicone, Neoprene and other elastomers.

02 / Engineering Plastics

Nylon Component Tools & Moulds

Tooling for nylon components is developed with material shrinkage, part geometry, dimensional accuracy, ejection and production requirements in mind.

  • Nylon bushes
  • Nylon washers
  • Nylon spacers
  • Nylon rollers
  • Nylon gears
  • Nylon guides
  • Nylon seals
  • Custom engineering plastic components
03 / PTFE

PTFE (Teflon) Tools & Moulds

PTFE tooling considers material characteristics, forming requirements, dimensional control and demoulding for reliable production.

  • PTFE seals
  • PTFE rings
  • PTFE bushes
  • PTFE washers
  • PTFE gaskets
  • PTFE valve components
  • PTFE insulation components
  • Custom PTFE components
04 / Sheet Metal

Sheet Metal Dies & Tools

Production-oriented press tools and dies for cutting, forming and shaping sheet materials with attention to accuracy and tool life.

  • Press tools
  • Progressive dies
  • Compound dies
  • Blanking dies
  • Punching tools
  • Bending tools
  • Forming tools
  • Drawing dies
  • Piercing tools
  • Cutting tools
  • Custom sheet metal tools
